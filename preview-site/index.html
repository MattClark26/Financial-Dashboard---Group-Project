WHY INVERSION MATTERS

Short rates above long rates can signal that markets expect weaker growth and future rate cuts. It is a 6–18 month warning, not proof a recession has started.

DEPTH + PERSISTENCE

A brief shallow inversion is different from a deep one lasting many months. The weighted model deliberately gives more influence to recent observations.

HOW TO JUDGE IT

Compare lead time, caught recessions and false alarms. A dramatic probability in one month is less informative than a stable track record.

DEFINITIONS

What the diagnostics mean.

These definitions connect the terrain to the performance statistics beneath it.

Z-SCOREDistance from normal

Historical standard deviations above or below the trailing baseline. Positive stress z-scores mean tighter-than-normal conditions.

SHARPE RATIOReturn per unit of volatility

Annualised average return divided by annualised volatility, using a transparent 0% cash benchmark.

MAX DRAWDOWNPeak-to-trough loss

The largest decline in cumulative wealth. It measures severity rather than typical week-to-week variation.
