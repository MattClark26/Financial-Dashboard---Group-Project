## Evidence standard

For each material contribution, add:

1. The relevant commit or pull-request link.
2. A concise description of the work performed.
3. The validation carried out, such as tests, screenshots or a clean-run check.
4. Any collaborators or reused work that should also be credited.

Commit counts alone are not treated as proof of a fair contribution. The record should show the substance, ownership and verification of the work.
